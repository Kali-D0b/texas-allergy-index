Allergy Tips

🪟
Keep Windows Closed

By keeping your windows closed, you can easily avoid allergens invading your home.

🚿
Post-outdoor Showers

Pollen and other allergens can cling to your hair, skin, and clothes, which is why it's important to shower and change clothes after spending time outside to reduce exposure.

🌅
Avoid Going Outside On High-Pollen Days

Check this pollen forecast and other updates on the pollen count in your area, before going outside.

😷
Nose Protection

Wearing a mask to protect your nose from pollen and cleaning out your nose with nasal sprays can also help prevent allergens from lingering in your airways.

This is NOT medical advice. These are just general tips and suggestions to follow, if you are having a medical emergency because of your allergies, please see a medical professional.
